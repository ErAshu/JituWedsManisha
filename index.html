सप्तपदी · सात फेरेSeven Sacred Vows

Around the holy fire, with Agni Dev as witness, the couple take seven steps together — each one a promise for life.

  1. 1

    पहला फेरा

    We shall nourish and provide for each other, and walk together on the path of righteousness.

  2. 2

    दूसरा फेरा

    We shall grow together in strength — of body, mind and spirit.

  3. 3

    तीसरा फेरा

    We shall earn honestly and share prosperity, and use it wisely.

  4. 4

    चौथा फेरा

    We shall love, honour and respect each other’s families as our own.

  5. 5

    पाँचवाँ फेरा

    We shall be blessed with a happy home and raise our family with values.

  6. 6

    छठा फेरा

    We shall stand by each other through every season, in joy and in sorrow.

  7. 7

    सातवाँ फेरा

    We shall remain lifelong companions and best friends — for this life and seven more.
